## Long Description (max 4000 chars)
```
When life feels heavy — a failing relationship, crushing stress at work, a decision you can't make, grief you can't shake — Gita AI helps you find the verse from the Bhagavad Gita that speaks directly to your moment.

Just tell it what's on your mind. In your own words. As you would tell a friend.

Gita AI reads your situation, matches it to the most relevant shloka, and gives you its Sanskrit original, a clear translation, and a few lines of personal guidance rooted in that verse — not generic advice, but wisdom shaped to what you actually described.

────────────────────────
✦ WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT
────────────────────────

Most Gita apps are verse browsers. You scroll, you search, you read out of context. Gita AI is the opposite: you share your situation first, and the Gita responds to you.

The guidance isn't written in advance. It's generated fresh for your question, grounded in the actual verse — so it meets you where you are, not where someone imagined you might be.

────────────────────────
✦ FEATURES
────────────────────────

• Describe your problem in plain language — no searching or chapter knowledge needed
• Sanskrit shloka with English and Hindi translation
• Personalised guidance drawn from that specific verse
• Bookmark verses that resonate with you
• Full history of every question you've asked
• 🔥 Streak tracker — build a daily wisdom habit
• Dark mode + Hindi / English toggle
• Daily verse notification — start each morning with a shloka
• Works beautifully on any Android phone

────────────────────────
✦ PLANS
────────────────────────

Free — 3 questions per day, forever.

Sadhak (साधक) ₹99/month — 15 questions per day, for those who seek more.

Gyani (ज्ञानी) ₹249/month — unlimited questions, for the dedicated seeker.

────────────────────────
✦ BUILT WITH CARE
────────────────────────

Gita AI is built by an independent developer with deep respect for the Bhagavad Gita. It is not a replacement for reading the scripture — it is a doorway into it.

The goal is simple: when you are struggling, the Gita should be accessible to you in seconds, not after hours of study.

यदा यदा हि धर्मस्य ग्लानिर्भवति भारत।
Whenever and wherever there is a decline in righteousness... — BG 4.7

─────────────────────────────
Download free. Ask your first question today.
─────────────────────────────
```
